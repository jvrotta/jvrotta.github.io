Privacy Policy

Galaxy Dark Publisher. Last updated: November 11, 2026.

Galaxy Dark Publisher ("the app") is an internal tool of the Galaxy Dark studio. It runs on the studio owner's own computer and is used only by the owner, on the studio's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it uses and stores that data, and how access can be revoked.

Who uses the app

The app has a single user: the owner of the Galaxy Dark channels. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect data from viewers, followers or any other person.

Data the app accesses

When the owner connects an account, the app receives an access token from that platform. With it, the app can:

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): upload videos, set titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and privacy status, post a comment on the studio's own videos, read the channel name and ID, and read view, like, comment and watch-time statistics and YouTube Analytics reports for the studio's own videos.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta Graph API): read the name and ID of the studio's Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account, publish Reels to them, post a comment on the studio's own Reels, and read the insights of those posts.
  • TikTok (Content Posting and Display APIs): read the studio account's basic profile, send videos to that account, and read the statistics of the account's own videos.

The app does not access private messages, contact lists, payment data, or the data of any account other than the studio's own.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the studio's videos after the owner approves each upload, and to show the performance of those videos in the owner's private dashboard. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone.

Galaxy Dark Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How the data is stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local folder that is never uploaded or shared.
  • Statistics of the studio's own videos are stored in a local database on the same computer, refreshed regularly, and kept only while the owner's account remains connected. When access is revoked, they are deleted within 7 days.
  • The app has no servers of its own and sends data only to the platforms listed above.

Cookies

The app does not place cookies on anyone's device. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

Third-party services

By using the YouTube features of the app, the user agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Data handled by Google is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Data handled by Meta is covered by the Meta Privacy Policy, and data handled by TikTok by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Google and YouTube: Google Account security settings (third-party connections).
  • Facebook and Instagram: Settings, then Business Integrations, on Facebook, or Apps and Websites on Instagram.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy, then Security, then Manage app permissions.

Data deletion

To delete all data the app holds, revoke access as described above and email galaxydarktv@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". The stored tokens and statistics are deleted from the owner's computer within 7 days, and the deletion is confirmed by email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
